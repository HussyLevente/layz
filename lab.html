A quiet revolution in reading

Large text clears the bar at three to one. Body copy has to reach four and a half.

Every letterform carries an argument about how a sentence should feel. Choose two that agree, and the page stops fighting the reader. Choose two that disagree politely, and the page starts to sing.

Contrast is not a matter of taste. It is a ratio between two relative luminances, and a reader with low vision either clears it or does not.

Caption, legal line, or form hint — the size where a pairing quietly fails.
